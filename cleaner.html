Voiceover Cleaner

Sannate chhote, saans halki, awaaz barabar. Audio isi phone mein rehti hai — kahin upload nahin hoti.

1. Audio chunein

File yahan drop bhi kar sakte hain. Video ka audio sirf tab chalega jab browser decode kar sake. 15 minute se lambi audio phone par bhaari process ho sakti hai. Decode ke liye poori audio RAM mein aati hai; chhoti files behtar hain.

2. Apni settings

bayan ki jaan-bujh kar ruki rukawat bhi kat sakti hai - sun kar check karo. Lambi rukawat ka darmiyani hissa katega; itna pause bacha rahega.

100–500ms ki halki, noise-jaisi saans ka andaaza; dheeme alfaaz bhi halkay ho sakte hain.

Yeh simple soft noise gate aur high-pass hai, studio denoising nahin. Awaaz ke saath chalti hiss poori nahin mitegi.

RMS-based loudness andaaza hai, certified LUFS/true-peak meter nahin. Sample peaks -1 dBFS se neeche rakhe jaate hain; inter-sample peaks ki guarantee nahin.

Pehle audio chunein.

3. Cuts check karo

Emerald hissa katega. Neeche har cut ko wapas laa sakte hain; phir Process dabayein.

4. Suno aur export karo

Pehle — original

Baad — cleaned (mono)

PCM 16-bit mono WAV: file badi hogi (~5.8 MB/min at 48kHz; stereo ~11.5 MB/min). Is tool mein mono WAV hi hai, compressed export nahin.

Audio save nahin hoti; sirf settings is browser mein yaad rehti hain.
