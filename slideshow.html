Slideshow Video Maker

Voiceover + scene images se Shorts banayein. Files isi phone par process hoti hain — koi upload nahin.

1 · Frame aur files

MP3, WAV, M4A, WebM, AAC; video ka sirf audio. Browser decode support zaroori. Lambi files ko chhote parts mein banayein (max 10 min / 150 MB).

Load par downscale; full-size originals hold nahin hote. Bitmap budget 96 MB / max 40 scenes. Redmi 8 par 6–12 scenes aur chhoti narration behtar hai. Order select ke baad neeche badal sakte hain.

2 · Look aur motion

    3 · Audio mix

    Speech ke waqt auto-ducking; pauses mein ambience wapas. Preview aur export ek hi offline mix use karte hain. Peak zyada ho to mix normalize hota hai.

    4 · Preview aur manual timing

    Scene card tap karein, phir scrubber / play ke saath mark karein. Pehli scene 0 par; aakhri end voiceover par. Duration badlein to baqi bacha waqt redistribute hota hai. Auto dobara karne se manual timing reset hogi.

    5 · Export

    export ke doran tab khula rakho, phone lock mat karo

    MP4: AVC + AAC support pehle probe hoga, hardware preferred. Fallback: realtime lagega. WebM ko YouTube par upload kar sakte hain. 1080p experimental — Redmi 8 par slow / hot ho sakta hai. Export files bhi RAM leti hain.
